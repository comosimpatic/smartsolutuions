Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can you start a project? +

Most engagements kick off within one to two weeks of signing. We run a short discovery phase up front so the team starts with a clear scope instead of figuring it out mid-build.

Do you work with early-stage startups, or only larger companies? +

Both. We scope engagements to fit the team and budget in front of us — a startup's first production system looks different from an enterprise integration, and we plan for that up front.

What does a fixed-scope engagement actually include? +

A defined set of deliverables, a timeline, and weekly working demos so you always know what's shipped and what's next — no open-ended hourly billing.

Can you support an existing in-house engineering team, not just full builds? +

Yes — a lot of our work is embedding alongside an existing team for a specific system, migration, or crunch period rather than owning the whole build.

Is the phone/computer repair side connected to the consulting side? +

Same company, separate teams. Repair is handled by certified technicians independent of the consulting engineers — see the repair page for pricing and turnaround.

What happens after launch — do you offer ongoing support? +

We stay on to monitor, optimize and evolve the system as needed. The exact terms depend on the engagement and get scoped before launch, not after.
